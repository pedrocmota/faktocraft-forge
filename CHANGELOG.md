# Faktocraft 26.3 — 0.0.1

> ⚠️ **Old worlds.** This version jumps from Minecraft 1.20.1 to 26.3. Your 1.20.1 worlds should open with everything in place, but this was tested on a small test world, not on a big base with machines running. **Make a copy of your world before opening it in 26.3.** Expect two changes: sulfur ore in the ground becomes plain stone, and the night vision toggle resets once.

## What changed

- Now runs on Minecraft 26.3 with NeoForge. Works with JEI, Jade and REI. WTHIT is no longer supported.
- The status monitor now shows the same information Jade shows.
- Sulfur ore is gone. Sulfur dust is made by crushing vanilla sulfur.
- Electric tools can be enchanted. Drills, multi-tool and hoe take Efficiency, Fortune and Silk Touch; chainsaws also take Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods; the nano saber takes sword enchantments. Unbreaking and Mending have no effect, since these tools never break.
- Drills and chainsaws were rebalanced. Iron ones match iron tools, diamond ones are 5% faster than diamond tools, iridium ones are 20% faster than netherite tools, with or without Efficiency.
- Vein mining: break one ore, log or glowstone and the connected ones come with it, up to a configurable limit.
- Quarries and foresters now work far from spawn, where they used to stop.

## Known

- REI is still in beta for 26.3 and prints errors about vanilla potion recipes. Harmless and not caused by the mod.
